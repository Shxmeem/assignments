# Business Letters: Complaint, Staff Notice and Job Application

## Introduction

A business letter has one job: to get a specific result from a specific reader. A complaint should lead to a new delivery date, a notice should be understood the first time, and an application should earn an interview. The three drafts below are written with that in mind. Names, dates and figures are examples and can be swapped for your own.

---

## a) Letter of Complaint to a Supplier

Rohan Mehta
Purchase Manager, Shreeji Foods Pvt. Ltd.
14 Market Road, Kochi 682011
23 October 2026

The Sales Manager
Greenfield Packaging Supplies
27 Industrial Area, Aluva 683101

**Subject: Delay in delivery of Order No. 4471**

Dear Mr. Rao,

On 3 October we ordered 2,000 corrugated cartons (Order No. 4471). Your office confirmed delivery by 12 October. Today is 23 October and the goods have not arrived. We also received no call or message explaining the delay.

This has caused us real trouble. Dispatch of our customer orders has been held up for nine days. To clear the most urgent orders, we bought 300 cartons from another seller at a higher price.

We ask you to do three things. Please confirm a firm delivery date in writing by 27 October. Please deliver the full order by 30 October. Please also take the extra cost of the 300 cartons off our next invoice. If the goods do not reach us by 30 October, we will cancel the order and use another supplier.

We have bought from you for three years and would like to keep doing so. That depends on deliveries arriving when promised.

Yours sincerely,
Rohan Mehta
Purchase Manager

## b) Email to Staff on Change of Office Schedule

**To:** All staff
**Subject:** New office hours from Monday, 3 November

Dear all,

From Monday, 3 November, our office hours will change from 9:30 am–6:00 pm to **9:00 am–5:30 pm**, Monday to Friday. The lunch break stays at 1:00–2:00 pm, and Saturdays remain closed.

We are making this change for two reasons. Several clients start work at 9:00, and calls reaching us in the first half hour have often gone unanswered. An earlier finish should also help those who travel home in heavy evening traffic.

Please keep the following in mind:

- The new timings apply to everyone, including the accounts and dispatch teams.
- Attendance will be marked from 9:00 am on the new schedule.
- If the new hours cause a genuine problem, such as childcare or transport, please speak to your manager by 29 October. We will look at each case individually.

Please reply to this email to confirm you have read it. If anything is unclear, my door is open.

Regards,
Anita Joseph
HR Manager

## Conclusion

Each letter above names the problem or purpose in the first lines, gives the facts that matter, and ends with a clear request or next step. The complaint sets dates, the notice states exactly what changes, and the application backs its claims with figures. Sticking to these habits keeps the writing short and the reader's response easy.
